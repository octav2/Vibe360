Q: How does the drop-off iPad selfie pod work?
A: The pod is delivered, set up, and configured for unattended operation (via Apple Guided Access) for up to 12 hours. Guests use it independently — no attendant required.

Q: How much space does a 360 video booth require vs an iPad pod?
A: The 360 platform needs roughly 2.5m x 2.5m of flat space to spin safely; the iPad Selfie Pod needs less than 1m².

Q: Do I need Wi-Fi at my venue for instant photo and video sharing?
A: No — sharing is via AirDrop or an on-device QR code, both of which work without venue Wi-Fi.
