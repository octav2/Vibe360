Local Roots, Luxury Experience Across South Bucks

Based in Buckinghamshire, VIBE360 is an independent local event hire specialist serving weddings, milestone birthdays, and corporate celebrations across High Wycombe, Beaconsfield, Gerrards Cross, Amersham, Marlow, and the wider Home Counties.

We saw that traditional photo booths produced small paper prints that were easily lost or damaged. We created a tech-forward experience: sleek iPad selfie pods with instant AirDrop sharing, high-energy 4K 360 slow-mo video spin platforms, and authentic vintage telephone guestbooks you can listen to for decades.
