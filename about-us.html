We Bring the Party Energy & Capture Every Memory

Vibe360 was founded with one goal: replacing outdated photo booths with modern, interactive 4K video platforms and nostalgic audio telephone guest books.

Local Roots, Luxury Experience

Based in Buckinghamshire, Vibe360 is an independent local business serving weddings, birthdays, and corporate events across High Wycombe, Amersham, Beaconsfield, and the wider Home Counties.
