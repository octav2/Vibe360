# 02. Homepage Wireframe & Copywriting Blueprint

## Section 1: Hero (Above the Fold)
* **Headline:** "Unforgettable 360 Video & Audio Guest Book Hire across Bucks & Home Counties."
* **Sub-headline:** "Instant 24/7 online booking. Zero telephone tag. Lock in your date with a £100 deposit."
* **Visual:** Full-width background video loop (80cm 360 platform in motion + close-up of retro audio phone being picked up).
* **CTAs:** 
  * Primary (Solid Button): `[ Check Date & Book Online ]` ──► Triggers Calendar Widget
  * Secondary (Ghost Button): `[ View Packages & Pricing ]`

## Section 2: Social Proof Bar
* Trust badges: "5-Star Rated on Google", "100% Date Guarantee", "Full Public Liability Insurance (£5M)".

## Section 3: The Problem vs. Our Solution
* **The Competition:** Slow email quotes, hidden fees, outdated print boxes.
* **Our Promise:** Instant online pricing, luxury 4K video overlays, retro audio voicemails delivered digitally within 48 hours.

## Section 4: Package Cards (Interactive Toggle)
Display 3 distinct cards with direct `[ Book This Package ]` buttons linked to Stripe checkout.

## Section 5: "How It Works" (3-Step Frictionless Process)
1. **Pick Your Date:** Check live calendar availability in 30 seconds.
2. **Secure with £100:** Deposit locks your gear and attendant for the night.
3. **We Deliver & Run Everything:** We set up, manage the line, and deliver 4K videos instantly via QR code.

## Section 6: FAQ Accordion
Address key objections (space requirements, power, vehicle access, cancellation policy).
