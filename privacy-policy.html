Privacy Policy

Vibe360 respects your privacy and is compliant with UK GDPR laws.

1. Information We Collect

We collect your name, contact details, venue address, and payment confirmation (processed securely via Stripe) to fulfill your booking.

2. Digital Media Storage

360 event videos and audio guestbook recordings are processed and delivered via secure online galleries. Event galleries remain accessible for 90 days after your event date before being archived.

3. Contact Us

For data privacy queries, please email us directly via our contact form.
