Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide when making a booking enquiry, including your name, email address, phone number, event date, and venue details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to confirm and fulfil your booking, communicate event details, and send a Stripe payment link. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Booking data is stored securely and retained for up to 2 years after your event for accounting and legal compliance purposes.

4. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Please email us to exercise these rights.

5. Cookies

This website uses essential cookies only to ensure basic functionality. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Contact

For any privacy concerns, please use our contact form.
