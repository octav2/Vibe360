# 01. Site Architecture & SEO Strategy

## 1. Domain & Core Positioning
* **Domain:** `vibe360.co.uk` (or selected brandable hybrid domain)
* **Core Offer:** Premium 360 Video Booth & Vintage Audio Guest Book Hire
* **Primary Region:** Buckinghamshire, Hertfordshire, Berkshire & Home Counties

## 2. Information Architecture (Sitemap)

/ (Homepage)
├── /360-photo-booth-hire (Standalone Product Page)
├── /audio-guestbook-hire (Standalone Product Page)
├── /pricing-and-bundles (Transparent Package Breakdown)
├── /check-availability (Interactive Booking Engine Widget)
└── /locations (Local SEO Hub)
├── /360-photo-booth-hire-high-wycombe
├── /360-photo-booth-hire-aylesbury
├── /360-photo-booth-hire-amersham
├── /360-photo-booth-hire-beaconsfield
└── /360-photo-booth-hire-gerrards-cross

## 3. SEO Execution Rules
1. **Schema Markup:** Implement `LocalBusiness` and `EventVenue` JSON-LD schema on all town pages.
2. **Meta Pattern:** `360 Photo Booth Hire [Town Name] | [Brand Name] | Audio Phone Bundle`
3. **Internal Linking:** Every town page links directly to `/check-availability` with a pre-selected location parameter.
